# 🎛️ KROMA - AI Music Post-Production Studio

**The Ultimate Audio Post-Production Hub for AI-Generated Music (Suno / Udio / Udio Alternative).**

🔗 **Official Web App:** [https://kromasound.com](https://kromasound.com)

## 🚀 What is KROMA?
AI music generators are amazing for composition, but raw exports often sound "plastic", flat, or robotic. KROMA bridges the gap between a raw AI export and a streaming-ready release. 

Instead of buying expensive DAWs, KROMA provides an all-in-one web DSP suite to reshape the contours of your sound.

## ✨ Key Features
- **High-End Stem Separation:** Powered by Demucs, cleanly isolate vocals, drums, bass, and melody to fix muddy mixes.
- **AIR Symphonic (Convolution Reverb):** Apply real acoustic Impulse Responses (IR) to your instrumental stems to create authentic stadium or cathedral spatial depth.
- **Studio Acapella:** Polish your vocals with high-pass filters and doublers.
- **RVC Persona (Voice Conversion):** Seamlessly replace robotic AI vocals with custom studio personas.
- **8D Spatial Audio:** Instantly pan elements for immersive listening experiences.
